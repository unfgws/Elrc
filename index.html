Lyrics (eLRC)

Three formats work, and you can mix them. Standard LRC has one [mm:ss.xx] per line, and the words are spread evenly across it. Enhanced LRC adds <mm:ss.xx> before each word. Word-timing rows put <WORD:start:end|WORD:start:end> (seconds) under a line row.
